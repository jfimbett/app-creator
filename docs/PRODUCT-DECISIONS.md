# Product decisions — SEC Analytics example (delegated mode)

**Scope.** This file records the product decisions for the **SEC Analytics example only** (the five milestones in
`milestones/` and the app in `examples/sec-analytics/`). It is not part of the generic App Creator toolkit, and a
new student app does not inherit any of these decisions.

## How the delegation worked

- **Brief:** [GOAL.md](../GOAL.md), the instructor's written assignment. §1 resolves "ordinary product and
  engineering choices using this assignment", and §8 asks a `delegated-product-owner` to answer the
  `product-interview` questions on the instructor's behalf, without starting a new interview with the instructor.
- **Questions:** written by `product-architect` (interviewer) in step
  [B10-interview-questions](../runs/bootstrap/B10-interview-questions/RESPONSE.md).
- **Answers:** given by `delegated-product-owner` (read-only) in step
  [B11-delegated-answers](../runs/bootstrap/B11-delegated-answers/RESPONSE.md), from GOAL.md, PROGRESS.md and
  docs/TOOLKIT.md.
- **Recorded by:** `product-architect` in step B12 (`runs/bootstrap/B12-decisions-and-plan/`), on **2026-11-03**.
- **Agreement:** under GOAL.md §1 and §8, the recorded delegation counts as agreement for routine items. It does not
  cover new scope, money, publication targets, personal data or secrets (docs/TOOLKIT.md §4). Those are listed
  under [Not delegable](#not-delegable-items-and-how-they-are-handled).
- **Documents built from these decisions:** [product](../milestones/product.md),
  [architecture](../milestones/architecture.md), [acceptance](../milestones/acceptance.md),
  [plan](../milestones/plan.md).

## How to read the table

- **Id** `Q<n>` is the question number from B10. The planning documents cite these ids as `(assumption Q<n>)` when
  they rely on a delegated assumption. These ids are separate from the build decisions `D1`–`D8` in
  [PROGRESS.md](../PROGRESS.md).
- **Question** and **Answer** are copied verbatim from the B10 and B11 records (by script, not retyped).
- **Basis class:**
  - **Explicit instructor requirement**: GOAL.md (or a recorded owner decision) says it.
  - **Delegated assumption**: routine and reversible. GOAL.md does not settle it, so the delegate chose a
    sensible default.
  - **Explicit + delegated assumption (detail)**: the requirement is explicit, but a detail (wording, a number,
    a layout) was chosen by the delegate. The delegate's own split is in the last column.
  - **Not delegable**: money, secrets, personal data, scope or publication. Recorded as a blocker, never decided here.
- **Source:** the GOAL.md section, recorded decision or research note behind the answer.
- **Delegate's basis (verbatim):** the delegate's own justification, copied unchanged.
